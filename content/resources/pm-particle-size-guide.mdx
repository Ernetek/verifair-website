# PM1, PM2.5 and PM10 Explainer

PM1, PM2.5 and PM10 refer to particles by aerodynamic diameter. Smaller particles can remain suspended and may move beyond the visible dust source. VerifAir focuses on turning these readings into operational context, alerts and reporting.
