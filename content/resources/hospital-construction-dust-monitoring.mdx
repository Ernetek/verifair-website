# Hospital Construction Dust Monitoring Guide

Hospital works can involve refurbishment, demolition, drilling, traffic changes and temporary barriers close to occupied clinical spaces. VerifAir supports visibility by coordinating Dustlight devices across construction boundaries, corridors and sensitive areas.

Monitoring should be designed alongside infection-control, facilities, contractor and environmental management procedures. VerifAir does not replace clinical advice or statutory monitoring obligations.
